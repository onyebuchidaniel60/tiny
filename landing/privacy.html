Privacy notice
for this public preview.

This notice covers the public landing page and the synthetic-data Android preview. It is a draft for an early-stage product, not a substitute for the school-facing privacy notice and DPIA required before a real pilot.

What this page collects

This static landing page does not include analytics, advertising pixels, account forms, contact forms or cookies. It does not ask for a name, phone number, pupil record or guardian contact.

What the Android preview stores

The preview uses deterministic synthetic pupil, teacher and guardian records. It stores local demo data in an encrypted Room database whose key material is wrapped by a non-exportable Android Keystore key. It does not connect to a live Firebase project, send real SMS or process real child data.

Links to other services

This page links to the public GitHub repository and issue tracker. Those services have their own privacy terms. Do not post real pupil, guardian or school records in a public issue.

Before a real pilot

A real-school pilot requires a controller/processor assessment, a Data Protection Impact Assessment, a school-facing privacy notice, lawful-basis decisions, retention and deletion rules, cross-border transfer review, provider terms and safeguarding procedures. None of those are claimed as complete by this preview.

Contact

Use the public issue tracker for non-sensitive evaluation questions. Do not include personal data. A dedicated contact channel will be published before a real-school pilot.
