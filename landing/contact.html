Tiny is an early-stage public project. For evaluation questions, technical feedback or non-sensitive pilot interest, use the public repository.

Public issue tracker

Open an issue at github.com/onyebuchidaniel60/tiny/issues. This is the only contact channel published for the preview.

Do not post real pupil names, guardian phone numbers, school records, credentials, private URLs or security exploit details in a public issue.

For a real-school pilot

A pilot requires a separate operator/legal process covering the school relationship, DPIA, privacy notice, consent/verification, data location, retention and provider budget. Those arrangements are not open in this preview.

For judges and evaluators

The build status section and the build log are the honest record of what is implemented, what is scaffolded and what remains blocked by external access or physical-device capability.
